01

Returns & exchanges

Items may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of purchase with proof of purchase. Items must be unused and in their original condition. Hygiene, safety, or custom-made items may not be returnable unless faulty. Please contact us before returning an item.

02

Payments

Payment is due at checkout. Accepted payment methods are confirmed in store. Prices are displayed in USD and may be updated without notice. This POS records sales but does not collect or process card or mobile-money payments.

03

Orders & availability

Product availability and stock counts are estimates and may change. We’ll contact you if an item is unavailable. Special or custom orders are confirmed with the customer before preparation.

04

Privacy

Customer names and sale notes are stored only in this browser on this device. Use customer details only to support a purchase or respond to a request. Do not enter payment credentials or sensitive personal information.
